Shorts Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Shorts Studio is a private, single-operator tool that uploads short videos to YouTube channels owned by its operator. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services. Shorts Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses. With the operator's consent, Shorts Studio requests:

  • permission to upload videos (youtube.upload), used to upload videos the operator has approved, with their title, description, tags and audience settings;
  • read access to the channel (youtube.readonly), used to confirm uploads;
  • read access to YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly), used to read view and retention statistics of the operator's own videos.

What it stores. A Google OAuth refresh token, kept only on the operator's own computer and never shared; the IDs and aggregate statistics (views, watch time, retention) of the operator's own videos, kept in local notes. It collects no data about viewers or any other person, uses no cookies or device tracking, serves no advertising, and shares nothing with third parties.

Deleting data and revoking access. The operator can delete the stored token and notes at any time. In addition, access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
